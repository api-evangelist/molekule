Winter dust is almost always caused by dry air and static electricity. But dry air and static don’t just happen; heating your home causes indoor moisture to dip below healthy levels. However, it’s possible to heat your home and maintain safe levels of relative indoor humidity by using a cool mist humidifier and other easy fixes.
