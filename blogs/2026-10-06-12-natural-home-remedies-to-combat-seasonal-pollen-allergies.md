Natural approaches to seasonal allergies range from well-studied to barely studied, and it is worth knowing which is which before you spend your money. This guide covers twelve of them, what the research actually shows for each, and where the evidence is thin. Used alongside conventional treatment, sensible allergen avoidance and air purifiers , several of these can meaningfully reduce symptoms.
