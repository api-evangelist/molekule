Your HVAC system runs for hours every day, pushing air through every room in your home. But if your ducts are dirty, they also distribute dust, allergens, mold spores, and bacteria throughout the home. According to the EPA , Americans spend around 90% of their time indoors, which means the air quality in your home can make or break your family’s healthy lifestyle.
