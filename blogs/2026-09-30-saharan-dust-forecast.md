Every summer, a wall of Saharan dust travels thousands of miles across the Atlantic and lands on the doorstep of Florida, the Gulf Coast, and the Caribbean. Most people only notice it when the sky turns hazy or their allergies flare up. But by the time you can see it, the dust is already in your air.
