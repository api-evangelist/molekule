If you’re shopping for a humidifier for your home and recently realized there are both ultrasonic humidifiers and evaporative humidifiers, you’re not alone. Most fundamentally understand what a humidifier does , much less the difference between the two. They both increase your home’s relative humidity , but the way they do it differs in terms of noise level, water quality requirements, maintenance effort, safety, and indoor air quality.
