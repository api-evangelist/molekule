Key Takeaways Chronic low-grade inflammation, known as inflammaging , is a normal part of aging but it is not fixed. Lifestyle choices have a measurable effect on your inflammatory markers. Research suggests measurable inflammatory changes can begin as early as your 30s and 40s, well before most people start thinking about it.
