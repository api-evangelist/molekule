If you work from home, your office is probably one of the dustier rooms in the house, and you may not be sure why. The why is pretty simple: electronic devices generate static electricity, dense cable setups trap particles, and there’s no nightly cleaning service that commercial buildings often receive. And while dust is often considered just a cosmetic issue, breathing in dusty air for 8 hours every day is bad news for your health.
