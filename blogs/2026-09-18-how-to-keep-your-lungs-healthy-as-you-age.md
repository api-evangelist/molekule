Key Takeaways Lung function starts declining around age 35, but lifestyle choices significantly slow that process. Regular aerobic exercise and deep breathing exercises strengthen your diaphragm and improve lung capacity. A diet high in fiber, omega-3 fatty acids, and vitamin D supports better lung function over time.
