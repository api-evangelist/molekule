Every summer, Florida gets a visitor from thousands of miles away. A thick plume of dust lifts off the Sahara Desert, travels across the Atlantic Ocean on trade winds, and settles over the state in a hazy amber cloud that dims the sky, coats cars, and quietly raises the air quality risk for millions of people. Some years it is barely noticeable.
