Key Takeaways Long-term exposure to fine particulate matter (PM2.5) has been linked to accelerated biological aging, reduced brain volume, and a higher risk of dementia. Air pollution and psychological stress compound each other at the cellular level. Both cause DNA methylation changes that accelerate how quickly your cells age.
