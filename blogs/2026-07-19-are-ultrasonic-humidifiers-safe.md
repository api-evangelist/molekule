The short answer is yes, ultrasonic humidifiers are safe. But when used incorrectly, they can be unsafe for kids, pets, and individuals with pre-existing health conditions. There are two main safety concerns with ultrasonic humidifiers: white dust caused from using tap water, and bacterial growth in tanks that are not cleaned regularly.
